Tax refund

WHO'S ELIGIBLE

You must be older than 18 years

Permanent residence or valid long term visa in a non-EU country

AT THE STORE

Inform the retailer that you want to export the goods to outside of the EU

Fill out tax refund form and have them signed by a store employee

Attach the original purchase receipts to the tax refund form

AT THE AIRPORT

Please allow enough time for the refund process before your flight departs

Stop at the customs office and present the following items

Tax refund form and original receipt from the store

Sealed & unused goods with price tags

Passport showing residence outside the EU

Valid flight ticket to a country outside the EU

After the goods have been examined,the export papers will be stamped

GETTING YOUR REFUND

Global Blue, TaxFreeWorldwide, or Premier Tax Free usually have seperate offices inside the airport Present your properly stamped documents and they’ll refund you in cash or transfer the refund to your credit card (usually within two billing cycles)
